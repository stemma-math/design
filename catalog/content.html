Avatar

A person is a circle with an initial; a group is a rounded square. A tone tells them apart. What is neither is drawn on the brand's navy, with a glyph or an initial.

.avatar--square--brand--{tone}
M E A NT GR C

Small avatar

At 24px, a small avatar fits within a small control in either density. It keeps its shape and tone, or holds a picture as a full-size avatar does. Give an avatar a name when it stands alone; hide it from assistive technology when a label beside it names the same thing.

.avatar.avatar--small--square--brand --{tone}
M E NT

Account row

A person link with an avatar, a name and a secondary line, beside an independent icon action. The link has one hover surface with even spacing around its avatar and text; the action keeps its own target. Secondary text can be quiet text or a non-interactive chip. Long names and secondary lines truncate.

.account.account__person.account__details .account__name.account__meta.account__action .avatar.chip.button.button--icon.button--quiet

Account row as a menu button

One button opens the person's menu, with the same avatar and text spacing as an account link. The name truncates and the trailing chevron turns while open. The optional matching width keeps the menu aligned with a narrow panel; it follows the row when the panel resizes. Keyboard selection and Escape return focus to the row.
