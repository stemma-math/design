Stemma design

How everything Stemma makes looks: tokens, faces, components, behaviour and the mark, with an entrypoint for each ecosystem. Every page here is drawn with the targets themselves.

Foundations

Colour, the six intents and the categorical tones, the faces and document typography, every scale, and the glyphs.

Components

Controls, content, feedback, navigation and overlays: classes over native HTML, and the custom elements that give them behaviour.

Brand

The symbol, the wordmark, the lockups, the icons and the brand light.

Using it

A page loads @stemma-math/design for the look, and @stemma-math/design-elements for behaviour. Rust, Lean and Verso, and the terminal have their own targets. Components speak of intents (neutral, accent, success, warning, danger, info) and scales; what a label says, and where things go, is each product's to decide.
