Foundations

Colour

Three tiers: the brand's ramps, the semantic colours that say what a value is for, and the tones components take. Every value is read from tokens.css as it is published, and every pairing is checked for contrast in both appearances when it is generated.

Intents

What a component means, not what it is called in a product. Each intent has a colour, a text colour for soft fills and outlines, and a colour for text on its solid fill. Shown on a card, in both appearances at once.

Intent Light Dark

Categorical tones

Tones with no meaning, only for telling things apart: people, groups, kinds. They take the same modifiers as the intents, as .chip--teal.

Tone Light Dark

Ink

Text from the most to the least prominent, and colours for placeholders, disabled controls, links and focus. Shown in the appearance in effect.

Surfaces, fills and lines

The page, the cards on it, fields, muted fills, borders, dividers and the scrim behind overlays.

On navy

Text and lines on the navy surfaces of the brand light, the same in both appearances.

Ramps

The brand's primitives. Components never use them directly; the semantic tier chooses among them.
