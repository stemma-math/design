Finite groups of prime order

Let G be a group of order p, where p is prime. By Lagrange's theorem the order of every element divides p, so every element other than the identity has order p and generates G (Theorem 2.1).1

Theorem 2.2

Every group of prime order is cyclic, and so abelian.

The proof in Lean

The statement, as Mathlib states it:

theorem isCyclic_of_prime_card {α : Type u} [Group α] [Fintype α]
    {p : ℕ} [hp : Fact p.Prime] (h : Fintype.card α = p) : IsCyclic α := by
  obtain ⟨g, hg⟩ := exists_ne (1 : α)
  refine ⟨g, fun x ↦ ?_⟩
  -- ⊢ x ∈ Subgroup.zpowers g
  simp_all [← orderOf_eq_card_of_forall_mem_zpowers, Nat.Prime.eq_one_or_self_of_dvd]
