What a sidebar needs: navigation lists with items that keep room around a leading avatar or glyph and truncate their label within the end padding, and folding sections; a small avatar that fits inside small controls; and a scrim, shared with the dialog's backdrop, for drawers and panels.

The application icons (ICNS, ICO, SVG and PNG from 16 to 1024 px) now ship in `@stemma-math/design` under `icons/app/`, for desktop apps to bundle.
