What a conversation needs: glyphs to attach, send and reply; links outside documents; unresolved references; small chips; posts; copy blocks; and the icons a page links from its `<head>`.

`.table` is now a grid whose head and rows share its columns. Set `--columns` as before; a column sized by its content now lines up across rows. Rows no longer have inline padding of their own: anything placed with `grid-column` inside a row counts the table's two edge tracks.
