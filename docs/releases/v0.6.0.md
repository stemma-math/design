Controls on navy panels can opt into `.brand-surface` in either page appearance: on-dark ink, readable selected options and chips, dark control fills, and opaque menus and popovers. Its tokens are checked for contrast alongside the page appearances.

Scrollbars follow the local surface with thin thumbs, transparent tracks and a stronger hover colour. The CSS brand light has gentler grain, adjustable with `--grain` independently of the gradient.

Navigation sections can have a trailing action outside their native summary, with hover and focus covering the heading row and separate targets for the disclosure and action. `.segmented--fill` and `<stemma-theme-switch fill>` fill a column with equal-width options; the theme switch's boolean `fill` property reflects the attribute.
